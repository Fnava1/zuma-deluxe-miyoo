ZUMA DELUXE - MIYOO MINI PORT (ONION OS)
=========================================
QUICK INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS (ON-DEVICE):

1. Copy the contents of this ZIP package to the root of your microSD card.
2. Copy and paste the following folders from your original PC game (Steam, GOG, or CD)
   into this exact folder (Roms/PORTS/Games/Zuma Deluxe/):
     - fonts/
     - images/
     - levels/
     - properties/
     - sounds/
     - music/ (containing zuma.mo3 or zuma.it)
3. Insert the microSD card into your Miyoo Mini and launch 'Zuma Deluxe' from the Ports menu.
4. The autonomous on-console installer will run on screen, optimize your assets
   into 'main.pak' in under 2 seconds, and launch the game automatically!
